Legal directory submission pipeline

Chambers Lab · Kuala Lumpur · 2026

Law firms submit long, structured entries to legal directories every year, and the work was done by hand. I designed and deployed a pipeline that does the first pass.

  • An LLM extracts each submission's data into a validated Pydantic schema, using the Claude API.
  • Five linked Notion databases hold the work as it moves from intake to draft to review.
  • Event-driven Python workers run each step when someone clicks a Notion button or a record changes.
The check
Staff review every AI draft before it is used.
Stack
PythonClaude APINotion APIPydantic

Citation hyperlinking for legal documents

Chambers Lab · 2026

Legal documents cite cases and statutes constantly, and linking them by hand is slow. I built a tool that finds the citations and links them in place.

  • A local LLM (Gemma, served through Ollama) identifies legal citations in the text.
  • The tool edits the document's WordprocessingML (OOXML) directly to insert hyperlinks.
The check
Existing formatting is preserved, so lawyers get back the document they sent in.
Stack
PythonOllamaGemmaOOXML

Citation-aware retrieval for Pillar

Jam & Raph · Singapore · 2026

Pillar is an AI legal assistant for Singapore law firms. A legal answer is only useful if a lawyer can verify it, so I built the retrieval (RAG) feature that ties each generated answer back to the passages it came from.

The check
Every answer points to its source passage, so it can be audited.
